---
title: "Moral Urgency"
pubDate: 2026-04-26
---

The world is full of extreme suffering. This is well understood by anybody familiar with the barbaric practices common in animal agriculture. You and I are sheltered from this reality. We only catch brief, voluntary glimpses into the horror show whenever we choose to look.

We'll never be forced to [live in a cage for months on end](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestation_crate). We'll never have our [tails docked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docking_(animal)), [teeth clipped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teeth_clipping), or [beaks trimmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beak_trimming#Welfare_implications) without pain relief. We'll never be [macerated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chick_culling), [lowered into a gas chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inert_gas_asphyxiation#Slaughter), or [boiled alive](https://actnow.thehumaneleague.org/live-shackle-slaughter/).

You and I can choose to ignore these atrocities. The victims don't have that option.

And ignore it we do. We go about our daily lives, paying attention to our work, relationships, and hobbies, while [~271 land animals die for food *every second* in the United States alone](https://animalclock.org/). It's understandable - no one individual can solve a problem of this scale, and it's not like watching slaughter videos is going to save the victims. So the question is, what do you do in the face of a problem this urgent?

I have watched more animal slaughter videos than most. Every time I do, I get a profound sense of moral urgency. Thus far, that has driven me to boycott the industry by not consuming animal products. Yet daily life has a way of numbing that sense of urgency, lulling me into complacency.
